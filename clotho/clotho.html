Values

clotho is a private, local-first digital wardrobe and interactive outfit studio designed to turn daily dressing into a mindful, creative ritual without uploading a single photo to the cloud.

featuring instant on-device AI background removal in 0.2s, a 7-slot interactive mannequin canvas, and a visual outfit calendar with style streaks, clotho gives you complete data sovereignty with zero trackers and zero cloud dependencies.
