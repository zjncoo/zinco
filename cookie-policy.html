01

what cookies are and why they are used

Cookies are small text strings that visited websites transmit to your device (usually to your web browser: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, etc.), where they are saved and subsequently sent back to the same sites on subsequent visits.

Cookies can be distinguished based on their duration and origin:

  • Session cookies: automatically erased by the browser when closed;
  • Persistent cookies: remain stored on the user's device until a preset expiry date or until manually removed;
  • First-party cookies: placed and managed directly by the operator of the website being browsed (in this case, zinco.cc);
  • Third-party cookies: installed by an external domain or server via embedded page elements (such as web typography providers, CDNs, or external scripts).

types of cookies used by this website

The website zinco.cc adopts a strict data minimization approach (Privacy by Design and by Default). The categories of cookies and storage used are detailed below:

These cookies are essential to enable proper browsing, core interactive functionality, and site security (for example, managing smooth transitions, menu states, progress indicators, and portfolio interactive items).
Legal basis and consent: in accordance with the Privacy Guidelines of June 10, 2021 and Article 122(1) of the Italian Privacy Code, prior user consent is not required for the installation and use of technical cookies, as they are strictly necessary to deliver the requested digital service.

2. Functionality and Preference Storage

Local browser storage (Web Storage) may be utilized strictly to remember user browsing choices (such as banner dismissal status or interactive preview states). These do not track personal profiles across the web and do not require prior consent when limited to these functions.

3. Complete Absence of Commercial Profiling Cookies

This website does not use any proprietary commercial profiling or behavioral advertising tracking cookies. We do not engage in ad retargeting, behavioral audience profiling, or data brokering.
