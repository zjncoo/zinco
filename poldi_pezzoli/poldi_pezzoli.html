CPP casa poldi pezzoli

video created for home-museum Poldi Pezzoli, located in the heart of Milan. part of the Design Workshop 2026, its objective was to display the variety and heterogenity of the collection of items purchased by the collector, Giacomo Pezzoli, through the years, from 1853 to 1879.

the communication style is defined by paper textures and mockups that aim to distance from classic collage style in order to give off the historic-archive feeling.
