3M Soluzioni Immobiliari — Corporate Identity

comprehensive brand architecture project that unifies a dual-sector enterprise: AM Decor (construction) and 3M (real estate), intrinsically linked by the shared DNA of their three founding brothers. the core strategy centered on creating a seamless visual system, balancing industrial rigor with corporate sophistication to establish a cohesive ecosystem. this alignment transcends conceptual theory; it is tangibly executed across all touchpoints, from large-scale architectural graphics to integrated digital platforms, creating a unified and authoritative presence for the group.
