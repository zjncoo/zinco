3M Soluzioni Immobiliari - Brand Identity

Real estate brand identity, particularly created for a family-owned business. Three Brothers came together to manage properties that did get renovated or built by AmDecor, a building firm owned by the same family. The Brand Identity features a minimalist 3M brandmark, window labels, open-house signs and website, used as a window to their properties listed for sale.
