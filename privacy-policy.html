02

categories of personal data processed

When you browse and interact with this website, the following categories of data may be processed:

A. Browsing and Technical Data

The computer systems and software procedures used to operate this website automatically acquire, during their normal operation, certain personal data whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet communication protocols (e.g., IP addresses, browser type and version, operating system, Internet service provider domain, timestamp of requests, HTTP status response codes). This data is processed solely to extract anonymous statistical insights on website usage, ensure proper performance, and guarantee network and information security.

B. Data Voluntarily Provided by the User

The elective, explicit, and voluntary submission of messages via the contact form (“get in touch”) integrated into the footer of the pages or via direct email to work@zinco.cc entails the acquisition of:

  • Your name;
  • Your email address (necessary to reply to inquiries);
  • Any additional personal data spontaneously included in the body of the message.

C. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This website strictly uses technical cookies necessary for core platform operation and multimedia asset delivery. No first-party commercial profiling or tracking cookies are employed. For detailed information on cookies, please refer to the dedicated Cookie Policy.

03

purposes of processing and legal bases

Your personal data is processed for the following purposes in accordance with Article 6 of the GDPR:

1. Responding to contact and collaboration requests

Data submitted through the contact form or via email is processed to handle and respond to your inquiry (requests for design quotes, project proposals, general inquiries).
Legal basis: performance of pre-contractual or contractual measures taken at the request of the data subject (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Providing this data is optional, but failure to provide it makes it impossible to respond.

2. Technical maintenance and website security

Browsing data is analyzed to maintain the technical stability and security of the website infrastructure, prevent cyberattacks, intrusions, or unauthorized access, and ascertain responsibility in the event of hypothetical computer offenses.
Legal basis: legitimate interest of the Data Controller in ensuring cybersecurity and website integrity (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR and Recital 49).

3. Compliance with legal obligations

Processing necessary to comply with legal requirements set forth by Italian or European Union regulations, statutory authorities, or court orders.
Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation to which the Controller is subject (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

04

methods of processing and security measures

Personal data is processed primarily through automated and electronic tools, adhering strictly to the purposes outlined above and applying suitable safeguards to preserve confidentiality and data integrity.

In compliance with Article 32 of the GDPR, the Data Controller adopts appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent data loss, illicit or incorrect use, and unauthorized access, including full-site encrypted communication protocols via SSL/TLS (HTTPS) certificates for all connections.

05

data retention period

Personal data is retained only for the time strictly necessary to achieve the purposes for which it was collected, in accordance with the principle of storage limitation (Art. 5(1)(e) GDPR):

  • Contact communications and inquiries: kept for the period required to process and manage the specific request, and subsequently for a maximum of 24 months for archive and professional correspondence tracking. If a professional or contractual engagement follows, data will be kept for 10 years in compliance with Italian statutory tax and accounting obligations (Art. 2220 Italian Civil Code).
  • Browsing logs: stored in server log files for a period not exceeding 30 days, unless longer retention is required by judicial or public authorities to investigate cyber incidents.
06

data recipients and international transfers

Personal data is never disclosed to unspecified third parties, nor sold or rented to data brokers or advertising networks.

Data may be shared solely with authorized third-party technical providers essential for the operation of the website (e.g., GitHub Pages for hosting, Google Workspace for email handling, and Adobe Systems Software Ireland Limited / Adobe Inc. for technical web font delivery via Adobe Fonts/Typekit, which operates without setting user tracking cookies), duly designated as Data Processors pursuant to Article 28 GDPR where required.

Where technical service providers have servers located outside the European Economic Area (EEA), particularly in the United States (such as GitHub and Adobe Inc.), international data transfers are governed by appropriate safeguards under Chapter V of the GDPR, including:

  • European Commission Adequacy Decisions, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF), to which relevant technical providers officially adhere;
  • Execution of Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission as an additional security measure.
07

data subject rights (articles 15–22 gdpr)

As a data subject, applicable regulations grant you specific rights that you can exercise at any time with respect to the Data Controller:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR): obtain confirmation as to whether your personal data is being processed, and receive a copy thereof;
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): request the correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Right to erasure / “Right to be forgotten” (Art. 17 GDPR): request the deletion of your personal data when it is no longer needed or if consent has been withdrawn;
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR): request the temporary suspension of processing under statutory conditions;
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format;
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to data processing based on legitimate interests;
  • Right to withdraw consent: withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

Exercising these rights is completely free of charge and can be requested by emailing: work@zinco.cc. The Data Controller will respond within 30 days of receiving the request, extendable by up to 60 additional days in complex cases pursuant to Art. 12(3) GDPR.
