about me

01

background & philosophy

communication designer, freshly graduated from Politecnico di Milano. Currently exploring visual identity, editorial systems, vibe-coding, and digital experience strategy.

my pillars are clear: clean edges, hard grids (which sometimes are deliberately broken to heighten creativity), and strict typographic alignments — defining a radical, minimalist approach to visual communication. Something, to be impactful and direct, does not have to be over-complicated.

currently focusing on culture-driven and socially-engaged projects — initiatives that leave a tangible, lasting mark on communities and public discourse. Rigor and creativity meet in the center to build identities and artifacts that are never generic or easily resolved; the deep research and conceptual thinking behind each project is what truly makes it distinct.
