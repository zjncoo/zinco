Fioreria del Lago

brandmark idealization and creation for a small flower design atelier on lake Como, specifically in Torno, a quite small town perched sheer above the lake. the brief was clear: create a distinctive identity that could deviate from the uncurated ones, typical of small businesses, in order to make it appealing to high-profile event planners and venues.

the brandmark features a specific flower species, typical of the northern-lake area: the forget-me-not (non ti scordar di me), which knowingly represents human bonds, memory and hope.
