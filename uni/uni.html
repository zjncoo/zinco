Values

uni is the native academic workspace for macOS, engineered to solve the chronic fragmentation of higher education portals by unifying class timetables, faculty notices, deadlines, and GPA projections into a single fast, distraction-free command center.

built on an uncompromising local-first and zero-cloud architecture, all data stays strictly on your device: featuring intelligent 24h iCal timetable normalization that parses messy university feeds, a low-overhead AppleScript bridge for Microsoft Outlook, and zero-copy Finder links so your study materials remain untouched in their original folders.
